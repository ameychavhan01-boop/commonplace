# Chapter Architecture — Episode 02: Bruce Kovner
**Title:** *Bruce Kovner — The Trader Who Imagined the World*
**Spine:** a political scientist who reads the world like a chessboard — and who learned, in one fifteen-minute phone call, that a brilliant view is worthless without the discipline to survive being wrong. The episode moves from the near-ruin of 1977 to a risk architecture built so that nothing he doesn't understand can hurt him badly.
