## Method, risk, psychology (all S01, Marcus's own statements — IC)
| ID | Claim |
|---|---|
| M01 | Best trades align fundamentals, technicals (chart confirms), and market tone (reaction to news) |
| M02 | Put 5–6× normal size on trades meeting all criteria; other trades aimed to break even |
| M03 | Never risk more than 5% of capital on one *idea*; correlated positions = one idea |
| M04 | Always use stops; decide the exit before entry (broker holds stop for large size) |
| M05 | If a trade feels wrong, get out immediately; "when in doubt, get out" and re-evaluate |
| M06 | Hold winners and cut losers — both equally important |
| M07 | Follow your own style; trading others' ideas combines the worst of both |
| M08 | Ask how many participants are left to act on an idea — use momentum indicators, sentiment, market tone |
| M09 | Market that fails to rally on great news → sell (soybean export report; reversed into a profitable short) |
| M10 | Plot equity; a falling equity curve means cut back; in losing streaks cut size, sometimes stop 3–4 weeks |
| M11 | Don't rely on "experts"/brokers; no lasting conspiracies — the market is bigger than anyone |
| M12 | Top-tier trading needs innate gift; competence can be learned; gut feel and courage essential |
| M13 | Balance outside trading is necessary to last |
| M14 | Stocks (from ~1986): high EPS rank + low P/E + room for market-share growth + industry cycle; prefers small caps; distrusts relative strength |
| M15 | 1988 view: trend following era largely over except under major imbalances or strong inflation/deflation; central banks lean against currency trends |
