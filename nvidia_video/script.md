## 1. HOOK (0:00)

It's 7 AM. You open ChatGPT. Nothing.
You try another AI app. Nothing.
Your stock app is flashing red.
And it all happened because one company, worth about three trillion dollars, disappeared overnight.
`[ON SCREEN: 7:00 AM · "$3 TRILLION" · "GONE."]`

This is a thought experiment. But every number you'll hear is real.
So... what actually happens if NVIDIA vanished tomorrow?
`[TITLE CARD: WHAT IF NVIDIA VANISHED TOMORROW?]`

## 2. WHAT NVIDIA DOES

First, what does NVIDIA even do?

It makes chips called GPUs.
Think of a normal computer chip as one very smart chef.
A GPU is a kitchen with thousands of cooks, all chopping at the same time.
And that's exactly what AI needs: billions of tiny maths problems, solved all at once.
`[ON SCREEN: GPU = THE ENGINE OF AI]`

So GPUs are the engines of AI. And NVIDIA makes most of them.
By most estimates, about 80 percent of the AI chips in the world's data centers.
`[ON SCREEN: ~80% · "ESTIMATED"]`

But chips are only half the story. The other half is called CUDA.
CUDA is the language programmers use to talk to NVIDIA chips.
NVIDIA says more than 11 million developers build with it.
That's twenty years of code, tools... and habits.
Remember that word. Habits. It matters later.
`[ON SCREEN: CUDA · 11 MILLION DEVELOPERS]`

In just the last three months, NVIDIA made 115.8 billion dollars in sales.
And 107.6 billion of that came from data centers: the giant buildings where AI lives.
`[ON SCREEN: count-up $115.8 BILLION → bar: $107.6B DATA CENTERS]`

## 3. HOUR 1: AI GOES DARK

So let's play it out.
Midnight. In our story, NVIDIA is gone. And every NVIDIA chip on Earth goes dark with it.
`[CORNER: 12:00 AM]`

Hour one. The data centers go quiet.
ChatGPT, used by nearly a billion people every week, can't answer.
Image makers freeze. Voice assistants go silent. Coding tools stop halfway through a line.
`[ON SCREEN: "AI GOES DARK" · HOUR 1]`

A few services survive. Google, for example, runs a lot of its AI on its own chips, called TPUs.
But most of the AI world was built on NVIDIA. So most of it simply stops.

And it's not just chatbots.
Hospitals using AI to read scans. Banks using AI to catch fraud.
Weather forecasts. Scientists testing new medicines.
All of it slows down. Or stops.

## 4. DAY 1: MARKET CHAOS

Day one. The stock market opens. And this is where it gets scary.
`[CORNER: DAY 1 · 9:30 AM]`

NVIDIA alone is more than 5 percent of the S&P 500: America's 500 biggest companies.
So if you own a simple index fund, part of your money was in NVIDIA, maybe without you even knowing.
`[ON SCREEN: pie chart, NVIDIA slice "5%+ OF THE S&P 500"]`

That slice just went to zero.
About three trillion dollars. Gone in one morning.
That's more than the entire yearly economy of Italy.
`[ON SCREEN: "$3,000,000,000,000" · comparison bar vs ITALY'S GDP]`

And it doesn't stop there.
Companies that make parts for NVIDIA. Companies that rent out NVIDIA chips.
Companies whose whole plan was "AI."
They all fall together, because suddenly, nobody knows what anything is worth.

Want a real-world hint of how that feels?
In early October 2026, NVIDIA was worth about 5.7 trillion dollars.
In roughly six weeks, about 2.7 trillion of that value disappeared.
Markets everywhere felt it.
Now imagine not a fall... but the whole thing, gone at once.
`[ON SCREEN: line chart $5.7T → $3T · "-$2.7 TRILLION IN ~6 WEEKS"]`

## 5. WEEK 1: THE GIANTS PANIC

Week one. The tech giants panic.
`[CORNER: WEEK 1]`

Before all this, NVIDIA said it could see around 500 billion dollars of chip orders lined up through 2026.
Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, OpenAI: they were all waiting for those chips.
Now those orders point to... nothing.
`[ON SCREEN: "$500 BILLION IN ORDERS" → stamped "CANCELLED"]`

So who steps in?
AMD makes AI chips too, and OpenAI had already signed a deal to use them.
Google has its TPUs. Amazon has its own chips, called Trainium.
So why not just switch?

Three reasons.
`[ON SCREEN: WHY IT'S SO HARD · 1 · 2 · 3]`

One: factories. Advanced chips take years to plan, and the factories that make them are already booked solid.

Two: CUDA. Remember those habits?
Millions of programs are written in NVIDIA's language.
Moving them is like translating a giant library... one book at a time.

Three: the full package.
NVIDIA doesn't just sell chips. It sells the cables, the networking, the software... the whole machine.
Rivals have pieces of the puzzle. Nobody has the whole puzzle ready.

So the price of every AI chip still available shoots up.
Renting AI power becomes a luxury.

## 6. INDIA: THE BIG SHOCK

Now, what happens to India?
`[ON SCREEN: map of India · "INDIA"]`

India isn't watching this from far away. India is deeply wired into NVIDIA.

NVIDIA has thousands of engineers in India, in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Gurugram.
They design chips and write software that the whole world uses.
In our story, those teams wake up... with no company.
`[ON SCREEN: map pins BENGALURU · HYDERABAD · PUNE · GURUGRAM]`

Then there's the IndiaAI Mission.
The government has made more than 38,000 GPUs available to Indian startups and researchers, and most of them are NVIDIA chips.
For example, Sarvam AI trained its biggest model on 4,096 NVIDIA chips.
Overnight, India's own AI dream loses its engine.
`[ON SCREEN: count-up "38,000+ GPUs" · "4,096 CHIPS"]`

India's big new AI data centers? From Reliance in Jamnagar to Yotta's planned cluster of more than 20,000 NVIDIA chips, they were built around NVIDIA.
Billions of dollars of plans, suddenly stuck.

Indian IT is a 315 billion dollar industry, with nearly 6 million workers.
Infosys, TCS, Wipro and HCLTech all build AI services for clients using NVIDIA tools.
Those projects freeze. Clients pause spending. And when clients pause, hiring slows.
`[ON SCREEN: "$315 BILLION · ~6 MILLION JOBS"]`

What about gamers? Here's a surprise.
Most of India's 555 million gamers play on phones, and phones mostly don't use NVIDIA chips.
So mobile gaming survives!
But PC gamers get a shock. Graphics cards become rare, and very expensive.
`[ON SCREEN: "555 MILLION GAMERS" · phone ✓ · PC graphics card price ↑]`

And the stock market?
When Wall Street panics, foreign investors often pull money out of markets like India to play it safe.
That could drag down the Sensex and the Nifty, and put pressure on the rupee.
Even Indian companies with no link to NVIDIA could feel it.
`[ON SCREEN: SENSEX ↓ · NIFTY ↓ · ₹ under pressure · "COULD"]`

But here's the other side.
If the whole world has to rewrite its AI software for new chips... who has millions of engineers ready to do it?
India does.
`[ON SCREEN: "THE OPPORTUNITY"]`

## 7. MONTH 6 TO YEAR 1: REBUILDING

Month six. The world starts to rebuild.
`[CORNER: MONTH 6]`

AMD and Google make chips as fast as they can.
Governments pour money into new factories.
Programmers move old CUDA code to new platforms, line by line.
And NVIDIA's former engineers, some of the best in the world, get hired by rivals almost instantly.
Because the knowledge didn't vanish. It just moved.

AI comes back. But slower, and more expensive.
For a while, the future is put on pause.

Year one. The map looks different.
`[CORNER: YEAR 1]`
Instead of one giant, there are five or six serious chipmakers.
More competition. A slower start. But a stronger system, one that can't be knocked out by a single blow.
`[ON SCREEN: one big tower → six smaller towers]`

## 8. THE LESSON + TWIST

So what's the lesson?
When the whole world leans on one company, that company becomes a single point of failure.
It's like a city with only one bridge. Everything works perfectly... until it doesn't.
`[ON SCREEN: city with one bridge · "SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE"]`

And here's the twist.
NVIDIA doesn't even make its own chips.
It designs them, but they're manufactured by another company: TSMC.
So even the company the world depends on... depends on someone else.
`[ON SCREEN: "THE TWIST" · NVIDIA → TSMC]`
